    Get Mira.

    Free for your Mac. Takes a minute to set up.

    Download Mira.dmg
    macOS 14 or newer · Apple Silicon and Intel
    1

    Drag it to Applications

    Open the downloaded Mira.dmg and drag Mira onto the Applications folder.

    2

    Let your Mac open it

    Mira isn't from the App Store, so the first time, paste this into Terminal and press Return:

    xattr -cr /Applications/Mira.app
    3

    Hover the notch

    Open Mira and move your pointer to the notch. Settings live in the menu bar icon. Updates arrive on their own.
