Ask Mira · new

Talk to your notch.

Press ⌃⌥A and say what you need. Mira answers out loud, sets timers, saves notes, plays music, joins your meeting and runs your Shortcuts. Bigger jobs run in the background, and Mira tells you when they're done.

Meeting agent · new

Before, during and after every call.

Five minutes before, a brief: what it's about and what you promised last time. During, notes. After, a recap with your to-dos in Reminders and a follow-up email ready to send, only when you say yes.

Get Mira.

Free. Set up in a minute.

Download Mira.dmg
macOS 14 or newer · Apple Silicon and Intel
Step 1

Drag it to Applications

Open Mira.dmg and drag Mira onto the Applications folder.

Step 2

Let your Mac open it

Mira isn't from the App Store. The first time only, paste this into Terminal and press Return:

xattr -cr /Applications/Mira.app
Step 3

Hover the notch

Open Mira and move your pointer to the notch. Settings are in the menu bar. Updates arrive on their own.
