MIRA · JOB HUNT

Install the Mira extension

Download Mira Extension.zip

It lets Mira fill job applications in your browser. You always check the form and click Submit yourself.
Takes about a minute. Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge and Arc.

1

Unzip it

In your Downloads folder, double-click Mira Extension.zip. A folder called Mira Extension appears next to it. Keep this folder: the browser uses it from there.

2

Open your browser's extensions page

Click the address bar, type chrome://extensions and press Return.
(Edge: edge://extensions · Brave: brave://extensions)

3

Turn on Developer mode

It's the switch in the top-right corner of the extensions page. (It only lets you add extensions from a folder. Nothing else changes.)

4

Click "Load unpacked" and choose the folder

Click Load unpacked, go to Downloads, select the Mira Extension folder (the folder, not the zip) and click Select.

5

Done

"Mira" now shows in your extensions. Open Job Hunt in your notch: it says Browser extension connected within a minute. From now on, Apply opens the job and Mira fills the form. Check it, then click Submit.

"Manifest file is missing or unreadable"? You picked a folder that has another Mira Extension folder inside it. Click Load unpacked again, open that folder and select the Mira Extension folder inside it.
Good to know. The extension only talks to the Mira app on your own Mac. It acts only on a job page you opened with Apply in Mira, never on other sites. It never clicks Submit, and it never sees your passwords. If your browser shows a "developer mode extensions" notice, that's normal for extensions added from a folder. Don't move or delete the Mira Extension folder, or the browser will turn the extension off.
